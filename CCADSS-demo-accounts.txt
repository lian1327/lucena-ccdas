 HOW A COMPLAINT FLOWS (who logs in when)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Citizen submits at /report  (no account)
 2. PACD (pacd.desk)            verifies + routes to an office
 3. Office desk (xxx.desk)      screens checklist, accepts as P1–P4
                                (or returns to PACD) within 1 working day
 4. Division chief (xxx.chief)  assigns an engineer / crew
 5. Field staff (crew/staff)    first response → inspection → work done + photo
 6. Division chief              validates the work  → citizen sees "Resolved"
 7. PACD                        closes the loop with the citizen
    City Admin = override everything | Mayor = read-only oversight
